比库巴蒂摩卡（bhikkhupātimokkha）是[[bhikkhu]]每半月在[[uposatha]]上诵出的[[ganthapātimokkha]]，以“大德[[saṅgha]]，请听我说”开头，分为五个[[uddesa]]：[[nidānuddesa]]、[[pārājikuddesa]]、[[saṅghādisesuddesa]]、[[aniyatuddesa]]、[[vitthāruddesa]]。其中收录[[pārājika]]四条、[[saṅghādisesa]]十三条、[[aniyata]]两条、[[nissaggiyapācittiya]]三十条、[[pācittiya]]九十二条、[[pāṭidesanīya]]四条、[[sekhiyā]]七十五条、[[adhikaraṇasamatha]]七条，合计二百二十七条。本条末尾的“戒条名称目录”逐条列出各[[sikkhāpada]]的名称，作为检索入口。

{{toc}}

## 定义

《疑惑度脱（Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī）》在《序释（Nidānavaṇṇanā）》中把[[ganthapātimokkha]]分为[[bhikkhu]]、[[bhikkhunī]]两部，并以开头的称呼和[[uddesa]]的数目界定[[bhikkhupātimokkha]]：“*‘‘suṇātu me, bhante, saṅgho’’tiādikaṃ pañcahi uddesaparicchedehi vavatthitaṃ bhikkhupātimokkhaṃ*”（以“大德[[saṅgha]]，请听我说”开头、由五个[[uddesa]]构成的，是[[bhikkhupātimokkha]]）。{{para|id=207-1231|title=kaṅkhāvitaraṇī 1231|style=reference}}

《疑惑度脱》先注[[bhikkhupātimokkha]]，再注[[bhikkhunipātimokkha]]：“*Yasmā panettha bhikkhupātimokkhaṃ paṭhamaṃ, tasmā tassa tāva vaṇṇanatthamidaṃ vuccati*”（因为这里[[bhikkhupātimokkha]]在先，所以先说这些来解释它）。{{para|id=207-1231|title=kaṅkhāvitaraṇī 1231|style=reference}}

## 五个诵分

《律藏·大品（Mahāvaggapāḷi）》在《布萨犍度（Uposathakkhandhaka）》中列出五种[[pātimokkhuddesa]]，前四种分别诵到[[nidānuddesa]]、[[pārājika]]、[[saṅghādisesa]]、[[aniyata]]为止，其余以听闻宣告；第五种全诵：“*Vitthāreneva pañcamo*”（第五种是详尽地〔全诵〕）。{{para|id=215-820|title=vinayapitaka 820|style=reference}}

《疑惑度脱》依此给五个[[uddesa]]定名：“*Tattha bhikkhupātimokkhe pañca uddesā nāma nidānuddeso, pārājikuddeso, saṅghādisesuddeso, aniyatuddeso, vitthāruddesoti*”（其中，[[bhikkhupātimokkha]]的五个[[uddesa]]是：[[nidānuddesa]]、[[pārājikuddesa]]、[[saṅghādisesuddesa]]、[[aniyatuddesa]]、[[vitthāruddesa]]）。{{para|id=207-1231|title=kaṅkhāvitaraṇī 1231|style=reference}}[[bhikkhupātimokkha]]本文的结尾也以“*Vitthāruddeso pañcamo*”（第五，详诵）收束[[nissaggiyapācittiya]]以下各部。{{para|id=207-521|title=pātimokkhapāḷi 521|style=reference}}

## 各部条数

[[bhikkhupātimokkha]]本文在诵毕时逐部总结。[[nidānuddesa]]之后是：“*Uddiṭṭhā cattāro pārājikā dhammā*”（已诵四条[[pārājika]]法）{{para|id=207-513|title=pātimokkhapāḷi 513|style=reference}}；“*Uddiṭṭhā terasa saṅghādisesā dhammā*”（已诵十三条[[saṅghādisesa]]法）{{para|id=207-514|title=pātimokkhapāḷi 514|style=reference}}；“*Uddiṭṭhā dve aniyatā dhammā*”（已诵两条[[aniyata]]法）{{para|id=207-515|title=pātimokkhapāḷi 515|style=reference}}；“*Uddiṭṭhā tiṃsa nissaggiyā pācittiyā dhammā*”（已诵三十条[[nissaggiyapācittiya]]法）{{para|id=207-516|title=pātimokkhapāḷi 516|style=reference}}；“*Uddiṭṭhā dvenavuti pācittiyā dhammā*”（已诵九十二条[[pācittiya]]法）{{para|id=207-517|title=pātimokkhapāḷi 517|style=reference}}；“*Uddiṭṭhā cattāro pāṭidesanīyā dhammā*”（已诵四条[[pāṭidesanīya]]法）{{para|id=207-518|title=pātimokkhapāḷi 518|style=reference}}；“*Uddiṭṭhā sekhiyā dhammā*”（已诵[[sekhiyā]]法）{{para|id=207-519|title=pātimokkhapāḷi 519|style=reference}}；“*Uddiṭṭhā satta adhikaraṇasamathā dhammā*”（已诵七条[[adhikaraṇasamatha]]法）。{{para|id=207-520|title=pātimokkhapāḷi 520|style=reference}}最后是：“*Bhikkhupātimokkhaṃ niṭṭhitaṃ*”（[[bhikkhupātimokkha]]完毕）。{{para|id=207-522|title=pātimokkhapāḷi 522|style=reference}}

本文没有给出[[sekhiyā]]的条数。《疑惑度脱》把它们分三组解释，组名中带有条数：二十六条威仪，“*Chabbīsatisāruppasikkhāpadavaṇṇanā niṭṭhitā*”（二十六条合宜[[sikkhāpada]]的解释完毕）{{para|id=207-2059|title=kaṅkhāvitaraṇī 2059|style=reference}}；三十条饮食相关，“*Tiṃsabhojanappaṭisaṃyuttasikkhāpadavaṇṇanā niṭṭhitā*”（三十条与食物相关的[[sikkhāpada]]的解释完毕）{{para|id=207-2106|title=kaṅkhāvitaraṇī 2106|style=reference}}；十九条说法等相关，“*Ekūnavīsatidhammadesanāpaṭisaṃyuttasikkhāpadavaṇṇanā niṭṭhitā*”（十九条与说法相关的[[sikkhāpada]]的解释完毕）。{{para|id=207-2142|title=kaṅkhāvitaraṇī 2142|style=reference}}三组共七十五条，各部相加为二百二十七条。
