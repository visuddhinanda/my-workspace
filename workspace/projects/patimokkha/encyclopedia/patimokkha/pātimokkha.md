## 词源与定义

《律藏·大品（Mahāvaggapāḷi）》在《布萨犍度（Uposathakkhandhaka）》中解释[[pātimokkha]]一词：“*Pātimokkhan ti ādimetaṃ mukhametaṃ pamukhametaṃ kusalānaṃ dhammānaṃ. Tena vuccati pātimokkhanti*”（“[[pātimokkha]]”：这是诸善法的开端、门口、首要，所以称为[[pātimokkha]]）。{{para|id=215-772|style=reference}}

《疑惑度脱（Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī）》在《序释（Nidānavaṇṇanā）》中把它解释为“极为首要”：“*pātimokkhan ti paatimokkhaṃ atipamokkhaṃ atiseṭṭhaṃ atiuttamanti attho*”（“[[pātimokkha]]”，即 paatimokkha，意思是极为首要、极为殊胜、极为至上）。{{para|id=207-1230|style=reference}}

《疑惑度脱新复注（Kaṅkhāvitaraṇī-abhinavaṭīkā）》则从“守护”与“解脱”两部分拆解：“*pāti rakkhatīti pāti, taṃ mokkheti moceti āpāyikādīhi dukkhehīti pātimokkhaṃ*”（守护的人称为 pāti；使这个人从恶趣等苦中解脱、得释放，所以称为[[pātimokkha]]）。{{para|id=212-710|style=reference}}

## 戒巴帝摩卡与典籍巴帝摩卡

《疑惑度脱》指出，[[pātimokkha]]的词义虽然只有一种，所指却分戒与典籍两种：“*Iti iminā vacanatthena ekavidhampi sīlaganthabhedato duvidhaṃ hoti*”（按这个词义它虽是一种，依戒与典籍的区分则成为两种）。{{para|id=207-1230|style=reference}}

称戒为[[pātimokkha]]的例子，是“*‘‘ādimetaṃ mukhametaṃ pamukhametaṃ kusalānaṃ dhammānaṃ, tena vuccati pātimokkhan’’ti ca ādīsu sīlaṃ pātimokkhanti vuccati*”（在“这是诸善法的开端、门口、首要，所以称为[[pātimokkha]]”等处，戒被称为[[pātimokkha]]）；称典籍为[[pātimokkha]]的例子，是“*‘‘ubhayāni kho panassa pātimokkhāni vitthārena svāgatāni hontī’’tiādīsu gantho pātimokkhanti vuccati*”（在“他已详尽地善学两部[[pātimokkha]]”等处，典籍被称为[[pātimokkha]]）。{{para|id=207-1230|style=reference}}

两者得名的理由不同。戒称为[[pātimokkha]]，是因为它使守护者解脱：“*Tattha sīlaṃ yo naṃ pāti rakkhati, taṃ mokkheti mocayati āpāyikādīhi dukkhehi, attānuvādādīhi vā bhayehīti pātimokkhaṃ*”（其中，戒：谁守护它，它就使谁从恶趣等苦、或从自责等怖畏中解脱、得释放，所以是[[pātimokkha]]）；典籍则是因为显明了戒：“*Gantho pana tassa pātimokkhassa jotakattā pātimokkhanti vuccati*”（典籍则因为显明那个[[pātimokkha]]〔戒〕，所以称为[[pātimokkha]]）。开头所说的词义两者共通：“*Ādimhi pana vutto vacanattho ubhinnampi sādhāraṇo hoti*”（开头所说的词义则是两者共通的）。{{para|id=207-1230|style=reference}}

由于典籍与戒相表里，《疑惑度脱》说它的解释对两者都适用：“*Tatthāyaṃ vaṇṇanā sīlapātimokkhassāpi yujjati ganthapātimokkhassāpi, ganthe hi vaṇṇite tassa attho vaṇṇitova hoti*”（这部注释对[[sīlapātimokkha]]、[[ganthapātimokkha]]都适用，因为典籍解释了，它的意义也就解释了）。{{para|id=207-1231|style=reference}}

## 比库与比库尼的巴帝摩卡

[[ganthapātimokkha]]分为两部：“*Taṃ panetaṃ ganthapātimokkhaṃ bhikkhupātimokkhaṃ bhikkhunipātimokkhanti duvidhaṃ hoti*”（这个[[ganthapātimokkha]]有两种：[[bhikkhupātimokkha]]与[[bhikkhunipātimokkha]]）。{{para|id=207-1231|style=reference}}

两部的开头称呼不同，[[uddesa]]的数目也不同：“*‘‘suṇātu me, bhante, saṅgho’’tiādikaṃ pañcahi uddesaparicchedehi vavatthitaṃ bhikkhupātimokkhaṃ, ‘‘suṇātu me, ayye, saṅgho’’tiādikaṃ catūhi uddesaparicchedehi vavatthitaṃ bhikkhunipātimokkhaṃ*”（以“大德[[saṅgha]]，请听我说”开头、由五个[[uddesa]]构成的是[[bhikkhupātimokkha]]；以“圣尼[[saṅgha]]，请听我说”开头、由四个[[uddesa]]构成的是[[bhikkhunipātimokkha]]）。{{para|id=207-1231|style=reference}}

## 诵出：布萨与五种诵分

据《律藏·大品》，[[bhagavant]]把已制定的[[sikkhāpada]]立为诵出的[[pātimokkha]]，作为[[bhikkhu]]的[[uposathakamma]]：“*yaṃnūnāhaṃ yāni mayā bhikkhūnaṃ paññattāni sikkhāpadāni, tāni nesaṃ pātimokkhuddesaṃ anujāneyyaṃ. So nesaṃ bhavissati uposathakamman*”（我何不把我为[[bhikkhu]]们制定的[[sikkhāpada]]，允许他们作为[[pātimokkha]]来诵出？那将成为他们的[[uposathakamma]]）。随后宣布：“*Anujānāmi, bhikkhave, pātimokkhaṃ uddisituṃ*”（[[bhikkhu]]们，我允许诵出[[pātimokkha]]）。{{para|id=215-770|style=reference}}

诵出的周期是半月一次。[[bhikkhupātimokkha]]的结尾说：“*ettakaṃ tassa bhagavato suttāgataṃ suttapariyāpannaṃ anvaddhamāsaṃ uddesaṃ āgacchati*”（[[bhagavant]]的这么多〔[[sikkhāpada]]〕，载于经中、含于经中，每半月来到诵出）。{{para|id=207-520|style=reference}}

诵出不一定从头诵到尾。《律藏·大品》列出五种[[pātimokkhuddesa]]，第一种只诵[[nidānuddesa]]，其余以听闻代替：“*Pañcime, bhikkhave, pātimokkhuddesā – nidānaṃ uddisitvā avasesaṃ sutena sāvetabbaṃ*”（[[bhikkhu]]们，有五种[[pātimokkhuddesa]]：诵出[[nidānuddesa]]之后，其余以〔“已〕听闻”宣告）；第二至第四种依次诵到[[pārājika]]、[[saṅghādisesa]]、[[aniyata]]为止，第五种全诵：“*Vitthāreneva pañcamo*”（第五种是详尽地〔全诵〕）。{{para|id=215-820|style=reference}}

《疑惑度脱》据此给[[bhikkhupātimokkha]]的五个[[uddesa]]定名：“*Tattha bhikkhupātimokkhe pañca uddesā nāma nidānuddeso, pārājikuddeso, saṅghādisesuddeso, aniyatuddeso, vitthāruddesoti*”（其中，[[bhikkhupātimokkha]]的五个[[uddesa]]是：[[nidānuddesa]]、[[pārājikuddesa]]、[[saṅghādisesuddesa]]、[[aniyatuddesa]]、[[vitthāruddesa]]）；[[bhikkhunipātimokkha]]少一个[[aniyatuddesa]]：“*Bhikkhunipātimokkhe pana aniyatuddeso parihāyati, sesaṃ vuttanayameva*”（[[bhikkhunipātimokkha]]则缺[[aniyatuddesa]]，其余依前所说）。{{para|id=207-1231|style=reference}}

## 教诫巴帝摩卡与威令巴帝摩卡

《疑惑度脱》在解释[[nidānuddesa]]中“[[saṅgha]]应作[[uposatha]]”时，区分两种[[pātimokkha]]。诸[[buddha]]所说的三首偈是[[ovādapātimokkha]]：“*tisso gāthāyo ovādapātimokkhaṃ nāma, taṃ buddhā eva uddisanti, na sāvakā*”（三首偈名为[[ovādapātimokkha]]，只有诸[[buddha]]诵出，弟子不诵）；以“大德[[saṅgha]]，请听我说”开头的是[[āṇāpātimokkha]]：“*‘‘Suṇātu me, bhante, saṅgho’’tiādinā nayena vuttaṃ āṇāpātimokkhaṃ nāma, taṃ sāvakā eva uddisanti, na buddhā*”（以“大德[[saṅgha]]，请听我说”等方式所说的，名为[[āṇāpātimokkha]]，只有弟子诵出，诸[[buddha]]不诵）。律中所说的[[pātimokkha]]就是后者：“*Idameva ca imasmiṃ atthe ‘‘pātimokkhan’’ti adhippetaṃ*”（在这个意义上，“[[pātimokkha]]”所指的正是这个）。{{para|id=207-1257|style=reference}}
